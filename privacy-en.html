Privacy Policy

Effective date: to be set on release · Moru Apps

한국어 · Terms of Use

1. Basic principle: your stored files never leave your phone

  • Photos, videos, files, file names, folder names and room names you store are encrypted with AES-256 and kept only on your device.
  • The Developer runs no servers and does not collect, transmit or view this data.
  • Your entrance pattern and work password are never stored in plain form, only as non-reversible key-derivation values on the device.
  • There is no sign-up or login.

2. Personal data we process and why

  • Ads (free users) — advertising ID, device information (model, OS), IP address, ad views and clicks. Purpose: showing ads, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud. Processed by Google (AdMob).
  • Payments — order number, product, payment and subscription status. Purpose: confirming passes, handling refunds and inquiries. Processed by Google Play.
  • Inquiries — your email address and message. Purpose: replying to you. Processed by the Developer.

Card numbers and other payment details are handled by Google; the Developer never receives them. Paid users are not shown ads.

3. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Personalised ads and the use of device storage for ads: your consent, collected through Google’s certified consent message when you first open the App. You can change or withdraw consent anytime in the App: Settings → Ad privacy settings.
  • Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and ad measurement: our legitimate interest in offering a free version.
  • Payments: performance of the contract with you.
  • Inquiries: our legitimate interest in answering you.

4. Device features the App uses

  • Photo and file picker: the App only reads files you choose yourself. It does not request access to your whole photo library.
  • Deleting originals: only when you choose “Move”, and only items you approve in the Android confirmation dialog.
  • Internet: used only for ads and payment checks. Stored files are never transmitted.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Stored files and settings are deleted from the device when you delete them in the App or uninstall it. The Developer has no copy and cannot recover them.
  • Inquiry emails are deleted 1 year after we finish replying.
  • Payment records are kept as required by law (5 years under Korean e-commerce law).

6. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell or share personal data. Ad and payment data is processed by Google LLC (USA) as described in section 2, under Google’s own policies and safeguards for international transfers. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Contact Google: privacy inquiry form.

7. Your rights

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • You can withdraw ad consent anytime in the App (Settings → Ad privacy settings), where shown.
  • For data we hold (inquiry emails), you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a copy by emailing us. We will respond without undue delay.
  • If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

  • Files and lists are encrypted with a different key per room (AES-256-GCM); keys are derived from your pattern with PBKDF2 (600,000 iterations).
  • Stored items are excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.
  • Screenshots are blocked, and the App locks when you switch apps or the screen turns off.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children (under 14 in Korea, under 16 in the EEA, under 13 in the US), and we do not knowingly collect children’s personal data.

10. Contact (data controller)

  • Moru Apps (South Korea)
  • Privacy officer: Park Young-mee
  • Email: moruappstudio@gmail.com

11. Changes

We will post changes on this site at least 7 days before they take effect.
